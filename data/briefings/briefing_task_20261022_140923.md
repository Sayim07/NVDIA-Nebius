

**Executive Summary**  
As of 26 Oct 2026, the Apollo microservice (FastAPI 0.111.0 / Pydantic 2.7.4) shows no newly disclosed critical CVEs in its core Python dependencies this week. However, the Project Titan frontend stack (Next.js 14) is exposed to three actively exploited vulnerabilities disclosed on 14 Oct 2026 (CVE‑2026‑96889, GHSA‑673q‑jm6‑r5xr, GHSA‑p3m5‑59mp‑jv5r). Immediate upgrade of Next.js to a patched release (≥ 15.5.28 or ≥ 16.4.1) is required; backend libraries should be updated to their latest stable releases to stay ahead of any future issues.
